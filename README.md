# 🎨 A Free Online Marketplace for Artists

## 📌 Problem Statement
Many artists want to sell their artworks online, but most existing platforms charge fees to create seller accounts. These websites also take commissions and require monthly or yearly payments. Because of this, many talented students and small artists are unable to sell their work online and are forced to depend only on social media, which is not reliable for proper selling.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution
To solve this problem, this project introduces a free and simple online artwork selling website. The platform allows artists to upload and sell their artworks without paying any registration or selling fees. Each artwork includes a **“Buy Now”** option for customers. The website is designed to give equal opportunity to all artists and remove financial barriers.

---

## 🧾 Project Summary
This project is an online artwork selling platform created to support artists by providing a cost-free digital marketplace. Unlike other platforms, this website does not charge any fees or commissions. It allows multiple artists to showcase and sell their artworks easily. The platform promotes creativity, supports young and independent artists, and encourages fair online selling.

---

## ✨ Features
- 🖼️ Artwork showcase for multiple artists  
- 💳 Buy Now option for customers  
- 🆓 Zero registration or selling fees  
- 🌐 Simple and user-friendly interface  
- 📱 Accessible on web browsers
